life at the margin

a design system for marius busen: essays, poems and small things made with care. paper and ink, lots of room, and a few small delights in the margins.

  1. paper, not screenwarm surfaces, a little grain, ink instead of black. it should feel like an object you hold.
  2. words firsttype does the work. images and colour serve the text, never the other way round.
  3. one accent, rarelyterracotta marks what matters: a link, a number, a note. if everything is red, nothing is.
  4. delight at the margina hand-drawn underline, a tilt, a spring. small, optional, never in the way of reading.
  5. calm motionthings settle; they don't bounce around. everything respects reduced motion.

data & contrast

chart colours are checked with a colour-vision-deficiency validator in both modes. data-1…3 (lake, terracotta, pine) stay distinct in any combination, so scatter plots are safe. pos / neg (ochre, dusk) form a diverging pair with a neutral middle.

type

Fraunces for display and reading: a soft old-style serif with optical sizes, so it works at 118px and at 19px. Inter for interface. IBM Plex Mono for dates, numbers and labels. all open-licensed and self-hosted.

6

reading

essays get a 34em measure, old-style numerals and notes in the margin. poems get more air, hanging lines and stanza breaks.

economics has another concept that more people should internalize: opportunity cost.the value of the best alternative you give up by making a decision. when you choose one thing, you're simultaneously choosing not to do something else.
